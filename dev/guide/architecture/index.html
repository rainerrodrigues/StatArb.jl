The econometric layers know nothing about orders, and the execution layer knows nothing about cointegration. They only meet inside the event loop.

The event loop

run_backtest moves a single monotonic Clock forward one bar at a time. At bar t it:

  1. accrues borrow fees and interest for the interval (t−1, t], applies that bar's corporate actions, then fills orders decided at or before t − latency using bar-t quotes, volume and depth (halted bars don't fill);
  2. checks maintenance margin and liquidates if it's breached;
  3. every trading bars, re-runs formation on [t−F+1, t]: distance screen, then Engle-Granger and Johansen, then pair selection, then Kalman calibration;
  4. for each pair, pushes bar t into its ring buffer, updates the Kalman filter and, every health_every bars, runs the health checks;
  5. turns signals into target positions and submits orders, which become eligible from t + latency;
  6. marks to market and records telemetry.

Guardrails against lookahead

  • Latency of at least one bar. ExecutionSimulator refuses latency < 1, so an order can never fill on the bar that triggered it.
  • Signals use only data up to t. The random-walk model trades the innovation y_t − F_t θ_{t−1}, which uses the prior state. The OU model trades the filtered spread ŝ_{t|t}, which uses bar t, known at t. Either way, the order fills at t + latency or later.
  • Checked data access. history and assert_past throw a LookaheadError for any bar after clock.t.
  • Perturbation test. The test suite scrambles every price after bar 800 and checks that the equity curve up to bar 800 is bit-identical. That check catches leaks the other guardrails can't, such as a statistic accidentally computed over the full sample.

Data hygiene

PricePanel validates its input:

  • timestamps must strictly increase;
  • prices must be finite and positive, and microstructure fields finite and non-negative.

Gaps either raise an error (missing_policy = :error, the default) or are forward-filled as non-tradable bars (:ffill). validate_config checks a configuration before any work starts and reports every problem at once, for example z_exit ≥ z_entry, latency < 1, a half-life bound longer than the window can estimate, or a leverage and margin combination that would start in a margin call.

Mirrored ring buffers

A MirroredRing with lookback W stores every sample twice, at columns p and p + W of a k × 2W matrix. That means the latest W samples always form one contiguous block in chronological order. window returns a plain view of that block, and the Johansen and Engle-Granger routines read it directly, with no copying and no wrap-around arithmetic.

Threading

test_pairs, scan_johansen, rolling_johansen and sensitivity_sweep split their work into contiguous chunks. Each task allocates its own workspaces, so the threads share no mutable state. BLAS is pinned to one thread for the duration of a scan (with_single_threaded_blas), so that LAPACK and Julia's thread pool don't compete for the same cores.
